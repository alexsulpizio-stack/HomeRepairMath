# Content Standard

HomeRepairMath is a tool-first site. Every indexable page must earn its existence by helping a homeowner make a decision, not by creating another URL for ads.

## Minimum standard for a calculator page

Each calculator page should contain:

1. A clear user problem in the title and introduction.
2. A working interactive tool or materially unique calculation.
3. A plain-language explanation of the result.
4. Visible assumptions and limitations.
5. Safety or professional-judgment boundaries where relevant.
6. Useful supporting guidance specific to the item or decision.
7. Internal links to genuinely related tools.
8. A unique title and meta description.
9. Canonical metadata and sitemap inclusion when indexable.

## Avoid

- Rewriting the same generic paragraphs around different keywords.
- Publishing unfinished pages merely to increase URL count.
- Inventing local prices or implying national averages are quotes.
- Date-stamping evergreen pages solely to make them look fresh.
- Hiding calculator assumptions behind unexplained scores.
- Adding advertisements to pages that are not useful without the ads.

## Low-maintenance rule

Prefer ratios, formulas, ranges, user-entered prices, and durable decision principles over frequently changing national price tables. Add external datasets only when they create enough user value to justify maintaining them.

## Update triggers

Review a page when:

- the underlying formula changes;
- a safety assumption becomes outdated;
- a cited benchmark is materially revised;
- Search Console shows a page answering a different query than intended;
- users repeatedly encounter an unclear input or result.

Do not rewrite pages on a calendar simply to signal freshness.
